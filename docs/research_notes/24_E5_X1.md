## 4. Claims
**C1 (BS): a mixture over centres is BMA, and it only interpolates.**
- **MECHANISM:** posterior component weight = w_k × marginal likelihood. With τ ≈ 1.1 pair-ability spread, 1–3
  labels hardly separate centres 0.3–0.4 logit apart. X1-bma therefore behaves like a centre near 3G/4.
- **EVIDENCE:** its regret curve lies between v1 and v1_1 at every δ. Its worst case (8.57) is the average of
  the two worst cases, not their minimum.
- **DECISION:** reject (rule 2).

**C2 (CS): extreme components do adapt fast, but they over-adapt on normal benchmarks.**
- **EVIDENCE:** the real-data cost is concentrated at B1 (+0.9…+3.4e-3), then B3, and it vanishes by B15. One
  surprising label moves mass to a ±3 component that an ordinary benchmark does not deserve.
- **COMPETING EXPLANATION:** the ±3 location or scale 1 is mis-set. ε halving from 0.2 to 0.1 halves the cost
  but keeps it ≥ +0.5e-3 in both regimes. A smaller ε would converge to v1_1 and to its worst case.
- **DECISION:** reject (rules 1 and 3).

**C3 (RT/CO): the worst-case and the mean pull in opposite directions on this grid of priors.**
- **EVIDENCE:** no candidate improves both, and the pre-registered table gives "stop the line". The
  Minimalist's objection to report 17's mix-0.2 holds again with fresh seeds.
- **CONFIDENCE:** moderately supported. The real-data side rests on 4 folds; the synthetic side is a Rasch
  resample.

## 5. Deviations
- Matharena cannot reach a 0.02 base rate inside the bisection bound |δ| ≤ 9: it reached 0.026 at δ = −9.
  The other three folds hit 0.02 / 0.98.
- The μ-grid stops at +5. A 0.98 benchmark (δ ≈ +7…+9) sits partly beyond the grid for every model. This is
  a shared engine limit, not specific to X1.

## 6. What ships / how it plugs in
Nothing changes: the v1_2 facet candidate should keep `prior=("t", 3, "half", "learned")` (BASE2).
If a later decision deliberately buys extreme-benchmark insurance at about +0.5e-3 mean cost, the cheapest
option is X1-t1 (one switch, ν = 1, worst regret 4.49 / 3.19). Enable it with
`from paiec.models import x1_prior as X; X.install(); cfg = dict(cfg, prior=("x1", ((1.0, "half", "learned", 1),)))`.
The prior hook is orthogonal to the facet change, because it only replaces `log_prior` on the μ-grid.
